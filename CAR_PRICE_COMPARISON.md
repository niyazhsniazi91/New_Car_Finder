## Important pricing notes

- The brochures describe the cars and their equipment; they do not provide a complete current dealer price list. The prices below were therefore checked separately against current CarWale and, for the Nexon, CarDekho city-price calculators and model pages.
- **Ex-showroom** is the published vehicle price before city registration, insurance and other delivery charges. It is the same quoted ex-showroom range for both cities in the sources used here.
- **On-road ranges** are estimated city-specific prices, from the least expensive listed variant to the most expensive listed variant. Variant availability, fuel, gearbox and trim can change which end of the range applies.
- The ranges are online estimates, not dealer quotations. Registration/tax treatment, insurance cover and add-ons can differ by dealer, exact address, buyer profile and selected insurance. Discounts and waiting-period or stock-related offers are not deducted.
- Use the linked city calculator for the exact variant you are considering, then request a written itemised quotation from an authorised dealer before booking. The estimates below are a snapshot, not a guarantee of prices on a later date.

## 3. Price interpretation and shortlist observations

1. **Bengaluru is generally higher on-road than Mumbai in this snapshot.** The gap is driven mainly by city/state registration charges and the calculator’s other charge assumptions, not by a different ex-showroom range.
2. **The largest price spread is between engine/gearbox variants and trim levels.** This is especially important for Curvv, Venue, Venue N Line, XUV 3XO and Nexon. A starting price is not a useful proxy for the top automatic/diesel/feature-rich variant.
3. **Fuel alternatives change both the quote and luggage practicality.** CNG models may have a separate tank/cylinder layout, different boot space and variant-specific availability; compare the exact CNG and petrol entries, rather than only the model-level range.
4. **The advertised equipment is not always standard.** Across the brochures, cameras, sunroofs, ADAS, connected features, premium audio and larger displays are often trim-dependent. Check the persona/variant matrix and the final invoice.
5. **Before comparing dealer quotes, normalise them.** Ask each dealer to separate ex-showroom, registration/RTO, insurance, FASTag, handling/logistics (if charged), accessories, extended warranty, service packages, discounts and any exchange/finance condition. Do not compare one quote that includes optional packages with another that excludes them.
